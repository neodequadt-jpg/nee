### Data format

- JSON: `https://footbolno.ru/data/league/{id}.json` (standings + metadata)
- CSV: `https://footbolno.ru/data/league/{id}-matches.csv` (all matches, filter by season column)
- Bundles: `russia-matches.csv.gz`, `all-matches.csv.gz`, `all-standings.csv`
